# Susu & Neko's Block World – P.2 English Adventure

A pastel block-world revision game for iPad. It covers the P.2 English textbook and workbook units, Grammar Time, reading comprehension and three writing tasks (My Favourite Place at School, Places like Home – Rules, and My Favourite Season).

- Islands: School Village, Grammar Gem Mine, Story Forest, Writing Workshop, Bee Garden and Exam Castle
- Writing Workshop: plan → build → write on paper with a timer → check on the iPad against the model
- Today's quest, a dream house built with gems, and two sticker books with 32 stickers
- Mock exam: Part A questions and Part B writing on paper

Made for home revision. Characters and images are AI-generated. Voice clips are AI text-to-speech; the device voice is the fallback.
Progress is saved only in the browser (localStorage).
